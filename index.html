How it works

Less Time Cleaning. More Time Enjoying.

Three simple steps turn a long gravel-cleaning session into a few minutes of work.

1

Disconnect

Turn off the air pump and release the airline connection. The quick-release coupling lets the slab separate from the airline.

2

Lift

Unfold the handles and lift the GravelLift bed out as one piece. Both tubes come with it. No scooping, no vacuuming loose gravel.

3

Rinse & Replace

Rinse the removable bed under a faucet or in a tub, then lower it back into the aquarium and reconnect the airline.

    Air pump sold separately. To keep the price low we leave the pump out. Most tank owners already have one, and standard air pumps are inexpensive and easy to find. The risers ship detached so the box stays flat.

    Installation guide

    1. Place the slab. Set GravelLift on the bottom of the empty tank.
    2. Snap in the risers. Click the two clear tubes into their base collars at the back corners.
    3. Connect the air. Run the airline from a standard air pump, with the check valve above the water line.
    4. Fill the tank. Add water and turn on the pump. Setup takes under ten minutes.

    Care & maintenance

    • Rinse: after lifting the slab out, rinse it under a faucet or in a tub, then lower it back in.
    • Diffusers: twist out of the base collar and should be swapped every few months as mineral deposits clog them.
    • Tubes & airline: replace cracked or cloudy tubes with a tube kit, and refresh the airline and check valve with an airline kit.
    • Pebble modules: clip on with mechanical anchors, so a single module can be replaced.

    Flow-control & fine-tuning

    Each airlift has a flow-control dial to set the bubble rate, and a swivel nozzle you can aim to set the circulation. A nozzle tip ring twists to adjust flow.

    FAQ and support

    Will GravelLift fit my tank?
    GravelLift 10 is made for a standard 10-gallon tank, with a floor of about 20 × 10 inches. Try the Tank Fit Guide, and measure the inside floor of your tank before you buy.
    How do I clean it?
    Turn off the air pump, twist the quick-release coupling, unfold the handles and lift the slab out with both tubes attached. The airline and check valve stay with the tank. Rinse the slab under a faucet or in a tub, then lower it back in and reconnect.
    Does it filter the water?
    GravelLift uses quiet, air-driven circulation, working like a built-in undergravel filter. Rising bubbles lift water up each tube, which draws tank water down through the pebbles and slots, under the plate and back to the riser.
    Do I need an air pump?
    Yes. GravelLift runs on a standard external air pump, which is sold separately. There are no electrical parts in the water.
    What replacement parts are available?
    A Nano-Bubble Diffuser 2-Pack ($9.99), Airlift Tube Kit ($14.99), Airline + Check Valve Kit ($7.99) and a Starter Bundle Add-On ($14.99). Diffusers should be swapped every few months as they clog with mineral deposits.
    Is GravelLift available now?
    Yes. GravelLift 10 is available now for $54.99.
    Does GravelLift remove all aquarium maintenance?
    No. It's designed to make cleaning the gravel bed far easier, but it doesn't replace general tank care.
